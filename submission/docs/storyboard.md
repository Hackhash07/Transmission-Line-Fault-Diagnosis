---

## Visual Design Notes

- Font: Body ≥ 18pt, Title ≥ 26pt
- Background: #F5F7FA (light grey) on most slides; dark blue (#1A355E) on title/conclusion
- Accent colour: #2196F3 (blue); warnings in #FF6F00 (orange)
- Figures: Full-resolution PNGs from `results/figures/`
- No stock photos; all visuals generated by the code
- Slides do not duplicate text that is already in figures
